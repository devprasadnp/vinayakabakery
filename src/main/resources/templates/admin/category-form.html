How it's used

Every food picks exactly one of these categories - it decides which accordion section it shows up under on the storefront, and is a filter on both the customer and admin product lists.

Hiding a category (unchecking the box above) keeps its products intact, it just stops showing on the storefront until you turn it back on.

Danger zone

Archiving hides this category everywhere. You can restore it, or delete it for good from the archive — only once no food is left in it.
